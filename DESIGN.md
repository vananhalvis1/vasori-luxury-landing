# DESIGN SYSTEM - VASORI

**Aesthetics:** Editorial luxury, Dark mode, Gold accents.
**Color Palette:**
- Primary Background (Dark): `#1A1008` (Rich dark brown/black)
- Accent (Gold): `#B8860B`
- Light Accent (Gold Light): `#F5E6B0`
- Text (Cream/Light): `#FDF8F0`
- Borders/Cards: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`

**Typography:**
- Heading: 'Cormorant Garamond', serif
- Body: 'Inter', sans-serif

**Vibe:** Sophisticated, premium, minimalist layout with subtle micro-animations and ample negative space.
